About the Event

Inspired by the magical universe of Harry Potter, this event blends technology with
storytelling. Participants are sorted into the four legendary Hogwarts houses—Gryffindor,
Slytherin, Hufflepuff, and Ravenclaw—to face an epic mission: defeating a dangerous
cyber virus named Voldemort before his centenary on December 31.Throughout the
challenge, teams will tackle core computer science and cybersecurity tasks—representing
seven Horcruxes—including cryptography, vulnerability analysis, memory optimization,
database repair, and secure coding. Join us to test your technical skills, collaborate under
time constraints, and compete for the prestigious House Cup!

”Programming is the modern form of magic. Every spell is a line of code„
